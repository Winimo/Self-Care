FACE

Welcome to the Face section!

Our face shapes determine which hairstyles, spectacles and makeup techniques will look best on us. That's why a certain hairstyle look great on your friend but not on you.

Even if you don't know your exact face shape, you can still find styles that complement your features. So, lets find out if cat-eye frames suit your face shape or that oval frame.

There are many different face shapes, but the most common ones are oval, round, square, heart, and diamond.

Determine Face Shape

Tie Your Hair Back & Face the Mirror.

Measure 4 key dimensions of your face with a flexible tape measure.

Key Measurements To Take

  • Forehead Width - Measure across the widest part of your forehead halfway between your eyebrows and your hairline.
  • Cheekbone Width - Measure across the top of your face from the outer corner of one eye to the outer corner of the other.
  • Jawline Width - Measure from the tip of your chin to the jaw angle below your ear then multiply by two.
  • Face Length - Measure straight down from the center of your hairline to the tip of your chin

Common Face Shape and Characteristics

Now that you have your measurements, we can classify your face shape.

DISCLAIMER: This is for informational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice.

  • Oval: Face length is greater than the width, with a forehead slightly wider than the rounded jaw and softly tapered chin.
  • Round: Face length and cheekbone width are roughly equal with soft, curved lines and a smooth, unangled jawline.
  • Square: Face length, forehead, cheekbones, and jaw width are all nearly equal, defined by a strong, angular jawline.
  • Heart: Widest at the forehead and cheekbones, gradually tapering down to a narrow or pointed chin.
  • Diamond: Cheekbones are the widest part of the face, paired with a narrow forehead and a pointed chin.
  • Rectangle: Features a sharp, boxy, and angular jawline with visible, distinct corners near the mandible and a flatter, squarer chin base.
  • Oblong: Features the same long, balanced proportions, but with a softer, more rounded jawline and a curved, U-shaped chin.
  • Triangle / Pear: Jawline is wider than the cheekbones and forehead, creating a broader lower face.
  • Inverted Triangle: a wide forehead and temples that tapers steadily down to a narrow, pointed chin.
