BODY

Welcome to the Body section!

Bodies come in all different shapes and sizes. That’s part of what makes each of us unique.

Some of us are curvier, have hip dips and narrow shoulders.

You may feel self-conscious about your body type, but it's important to remember that all bodies are beautiful in their own way.

In this section, we will help you determine your body shape and provide tips on how to dress for your body type.

Determine your body shape

Let us first find out your body shape.

You can determine your body shape by taking four key measurements—shoulders, bust, waist, and hips—and comparing their proportions to each other.

To get the most accurate measurements, it is best to have someone else measure you.
If you are measuring yourself, make sure to use a flexible measuring tape and stand in front of a mirror to ensure that the tape is level and not twisted.

It also advisable to wear tight-fitting clothes or undergarments while taking the measurements.

Classify your body shape

Now that you have your measurements, we can classify your body shape.

DISCLAIMER: This is for informational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice.

Rectangle

It also known as H, or straight body shape.

This is when your waist measurements are about the same as your hip or bust, and your shoulders and hips are about the same width

Hourglass

It also known as X body shape.

When your hips and bust are nearly equal in size and you have a well-defined waist that’s narrower than both.

Your legs and upper body are probably considered proportionate.

Your shoulders may be slightly rounded, and you most likely have a rounded buttocks.

Pear

It also known as A body shape.

With this shape, your shoulders and bust are narrower than your hips.

You probably have slim arms and a fairly defined waist. Your waist most likely slopes out to your hips.

Inverted Triangle

It also known as V body shape.

This is when your shoulders and bust are larger than your relatively narrow hips

Apple

It also known as O body shape.

This is when your bust is larger than the rest of your body, your hips are narrow, and your midsection is fuller.

Diamond

This is when you have broader hips than shoulders, a narrow bust, and a fuller waistline.
