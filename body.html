BODY

Welcome to the Body section!

We often at times dress our bodies in clothes that don't flatter us.

We might see someone with gorgeous attire and buy it but looks awful.

It is due to the fact that we don't dress in our body shape.

Determine your body shape

Let us first find out our body shape.

DISCLAIMER: Don't measure yourself because you can get incorrect measurement leading to inaccurate results.

A full-length picture is better since it gives you a more reliastic representation of how your actual body looks.

Here is what you need to do:

  1. Wear simple and tight-fitting clothes to get accurate results and classification.
  2. Find a spot in your room with sufficient space and lighting.
  3. Place your phone at eye-level, hold it straight and vertical to the ground.
    You may use a tripod.
  4. Turn on the back camera and start the video recording button.
  5. Stand about 3 meters away from the camera.
  6. Stand straight with arms on the side. Take a shot from the front and side of the body.

    7. Take one or two steps closer to the camera to get a picture of how you would look in a normal social distance.

    Do not stand close to the camera since the camera lens might distort your body proportions.
    It tends to make upper body appear wider and lower body appear narrower.

  7. Select the frame where you are positioned to stand still, screenshot itand crop out the the extra spaces behind.

Classify your body shape

Now that you have your pictures, we will look at the front, side and shoulder areas to determine your body shape.

Front View

We will analyze the widest region of your bust and hips.

H

H body shape also known as rectangle body shape.

The width of the bust and hips are similar, and the waist is not significantly narrower than the hips.

X

X body shape also known as hourglass body shape.

The width of the bust and hips are similar, and the waist is significantly narrower than the hips.

A

A body shape also known as pear body shape.

The width of the hips is greater than the bust, and the waist is significantly narrower than the hips.

V

V body shape also known as inverted triangle body shape.

The width of the bust is greater than the hips, and the waist is notsignificantly narrower than the hips.
